# Research route registry

This ledger groups routes by mathematical mechanism, not by wording.  A route
is promoted only after separate checks of correctness, novelty, and practical
importance.  `open` means that a concrete theorem/experiment is still being
worked out; `blocked` records the missing lemma or evidence needed to reopen it.

## Promotion rule

A central route must have (i) a complete proof with assumption-removal
counterexamples, (ii) a variable-by-variable prior-art comparison, (iii) an
independent attempt to break the theorem, and (iv) a real or documented-system
experiment in which an observationally identical model leads to a different
structural action and a measured consequence.
