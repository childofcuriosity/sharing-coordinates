# Pretrained-model extension: delivery judgment

The completed study supports a scoped public preprint about optimizer-response identifiability, with substantially broader pretrained-model evidence. It does **not** establish a new LLM capability improvement, a universally transferable trust certificate, or a compression advantage. The theoretical claim remains primary; fixed isometric embedding connects it to the tested additive module.

Nine public models span Pythia 160M/1B/2.8B, Qwen3 0.6B/1.7B/4B/8B and SmolLM2 360M/1.7B. All three adaptation seeds complete: 27 evaluation checkpoints, 1,620 query/noise/domain cases and 4,860 method outputs. Four separate calibration checkpoints fix policies. Llama access was unavailable and is explicitly absent, with the public fallback declared before outcomes.

Joint fitting returns all 1,080 sufficient-rank cases with maximum error 0.0337753; 540 one-query cases are skipped and rejected. Noiseless q4 worst error is 8.31e-13. These are controlled FP64 factor responses driven by designed, random or measured real-model gradients, not full-network FP64 or AdamW trajectory recovery.

The combined joint-fit policy accepts all 1,080 sufficient-rank cases with no observed bad output; this cannot establish rare-failure control. For projected spectral recovery, combined rejection accepts 43 bad cases among 1,056 versus 20 among 1,037 for residual-only, including an accepted error of 6.19. The earlier adverse synthetic results remain in the manuscript. All-reject spectral calibration and the strict gate's numerical effect are explicit.

All 45 structural actions complete at nine declared checkpoints. Recovered routers match native partitions, but effective-parameter clustering also matches all nine. This is no evidence of incremental structural-selection value. Identical-action Pythia repeats have NLL variation up to 0.00152, preventing small numerical differences from being treated as structural gains.

The 72 registered artificial-factor stress cases show router-rank deterioration amplifying error, without being counted as extra trained models. The isolated CPU replay covers 81 selected cases and 243 estimator outputs, with matching acceptance decisions and maximum estimate difference 1.39e-10. A cross-environment unit-test tolerance correction is documented separately; raw estimators, thresholds and results are unchanged.

The added formal bridge proves the response pullback/pushforward identity for a known fixed isometry. It does not remove the original nondegeneracy, access, metric or dimension assumptions. We keep the earlier theorem release under `releases/2026-09-25-theory/`, the ICLR2027 template and author placeholder. No upload is performed.
