This ledger separates a paper's actual claim from our interpretation.  Links
point to primary papers, official proceedings, or author repositories.  It is
not evidence that the listed practices are widespread.

## Scope conclusion

This audited corpus contains a small number of concrete
coordinate-to-structure actions and does not support a broad claim that
learned sharing maps generally purport to recover a true historical graph.
A defensible paper should audit those actions and their
decision risk.  Compression-only or explicitly discrete methods must not be
recast as offenders merely because they draw a sharing diagram.

The E2LoRA audit sharpens this boundary.  It is a genuine data-driven
structure-to-decision example, but its structure is computed from task
gradients before adapter fine-tuning and is validated as an allocation rule.
It is therefore neither evidence about ambiguity of learned factor
coordinates nor evidence of a historical-graph recovery claim.

## Public artifact status for ASLoRA

As of 2026-08-22, searches of the arXiv record, formal article page, author
names plus GitHub, and Hugging Face did not locate an author-released codebase
or checkpoint.  This is a dated "not located" statement, not proof that no
artifact exists.  Any experiment in this repository must therefore be called
a documented reimplementation and witness, not an official reproduction.
